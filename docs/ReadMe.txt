AG Bear 2.0
===========

Two programs starring AG Bear, for an AGA Amiga (A1200 / A4000) or an Amiga
with a graphics card, AmigaOS 3.0 or newer.  Double-click an icon to start.


AG Bear Pet
-----------

Click (or press fire) on the title screen, then look after AG Bear!  Keep
his five bars happy (along the bottom of the screen):

  apple   food      heart  fun      Zzz  energy
  bubble  clean     cross  health

The buttons change with where you are.  Point at a button to see what it does.

  Home     Feed, Bath, Nap on the sofa, Medicine, Balloon memory game,
           Dress up, Go out, Stats and menu
  Garden   Fetch (needs a ball), Picnic apple, Dance, Feed, Dress up
  Park     Say hello, Play together (tag!), Give a present, Swings,
           Catch the honey game, Dance
  Shop     Honey's shop: honey, cake, medicine, a ball, presents,
           sunglasses and a party hat

Friends: Rosie, Pip, Starfish, Fudgie Arms, Original Grandmaw and HuliHoop
play in the park (one or two at a time), Sid the Seal splashes in the park
pond, and Honey runs the shop.  Say hello, play tag and give presents to
fill their hearts.  Best friends (3 hearts or
more) sometimes knock on AG Bear's door to visit.

Coins: win them in the Catch game (park) and the Balloon game (home), from
playing tag, from presents, and as pocket money once a day.

The golden crown: catch 25 things in one Catch game, or reach level 7 in the
Balloon game.

Click AG Bear to tickle him.  Joystick: left/right picks a button, fire
presses it; in the Catch game the joystick (or the mouse) moves AG Bear.
Keys 1-8 press the buttons; Esc saves and quits.

AG Bear is saved in AGBear.save next to the program, or in ENVARC: when the
program's drawer is read-only.  He never gets ill for good - medicine from
the shop and some looking after always makes him better.


AG Bear Talker
--------------

Type something in the box at the bottom and press Return (or click Say):
AG Bear says it.

On the right:  Who is talking (AG Bear, Party Bear, Professor Bear, Ted,
Sid the Seal, Starfish, Fudgie Arms, Original Grandmaw or HuliHoop), Hat, Glasses, Bow tie, Moustache, Fur colour (try Rainbow!),
Backdrop (try Disco and Space!) and Voice.  Click the right side of a row for
the next choice, the left side (or the right mouse button) to go back.

The buttons:   tell a joke, sing a song, surprise outfit, bear noises,
               take a photo (saved in RAM: as an IFF picture - open it with
               MultiView), and bye.

Leave him alone for a while and he will entertain himself.


Speech
------

With narrator.device and translator.library installed (they came with
Workbench 1.x and 2.0) the bears talk with the narrator.  Without them they
talk in real AG Bear noises.  In the Pet, Stats and menu > Voice switches the
narrator off.
